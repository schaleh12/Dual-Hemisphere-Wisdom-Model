# LMFT-Lab — Design Decisions

## D033: Clean-Slate Reset at Commit #36 and 12-Leaf Constant-Prompt Architecture (2026-10-23)

- **Context**: Previous iterations after Commit #36 suffered from bloated bureaucratic documentation and rigged test rubrics. We reset the repository to Commit #36 (`b41e485`), archived the 1,480 lines of dense startup logs into `docs/archive/` so they do not pollute future AI context windows, and locked in strict Anti-Rigging and Context Hygiene rules in `AGENTS.md`.
- **Design Choice**: Instead of stuffing every learned rule into the `Qwen3-4B` prompt (which would choke the 4B model as rules grow), `Qwen3-4B` always scores the **same 12 foundational `Cultural` and `Philosophical` leaves**. Python then evaluates the growing list of fuzzy rules (`FuzzyConceptRule`) against those 12 numbers in microseconds.
- **Status**: Implemented in `src/lmft_lab/reflection_classifier.py` with unit tests in `tests/test_reflection_classifier.py`.

## D034: Require All Rule Traits to Match (`min`) and Benchmark 7-Concept Scaling (2026-10-23)

- **Context**: In our first 5-sentence live test, averaging a rule's traits (`mean`) allowed a peaceful hermit to match 2 out of 3 traits of an armed gang (`2/5` clear matches).
- **Design Choice**: Changed `FuzzyConceptRule.evaluate()` to use standard fuzzy `AND` (`min` similarity across a rule's required traits), so missing any key trait (such as `chaos`) drops the rule's score.
- **Result**: Improved the 5-sentence test from **`2/5` to `5/5` clear matches**. In a larger 7-concept (14-sentence) stress test (`experiments/run_feasibility_scaling_study.py`), rule evaluation scaled to **1,000 rules in 23.6 milliseconds**, and the tree generalized cleanly to **4/7** unseen sentences (`5/7` top-1) while exposing two clear next-step bottlenecks: 4B-vs-9B leaf scale alignment and single-example incidental traits.

## D035: Isolated Multi-Tier Reverse-Abstraction (`Tier 1 -> Tier 2 -> Tier 3`) (2026-10-23)

- **Context**: When we tried adding newly learned child leaves directly into the same `Qwen3-4B` prompt as the 12 founding leaves, `Qwen3-4B` shifted the numbers it gave to the original 12 leaves, hurting accuracy (`2/7` unseen matches).
- **Design Choice**: Keep **Tier 1** (the 12 founding `Cultural` and `Philosophical` leaves) in a locked, unchanging prompt, and score learned child leaves (`LearnedLeaf` at **Tier 2** and **Tier 3**) in a separate focused pass (`make_tier2_child_request`). When a deeper tier rule fires ($\ge 0.50$), Specificity Inhibition discounts shallower parent/trunk rules by `1.0 - 0.5 * max_deeper` so specific sub-classifications cleanly supersede broad parent rules.
- **Result**: Raised the 7-concept stress test (`outputs/feasibility_scaling_report.json`) to **`6/6` Teacher rules accepted**, **`7/7` top-1 correct (`6/7` clear matches)** on unseen test sentences, and verified automatic **Tier-2 $\rightarrow$ Tier-3** sub-branch splitting in `outputs/continuous_tiered_report.json`.

## D036: Continuous-State Sequential Memory Across Experiential Leaves (2026-10-23)

- **Context**: Previously, the tree reset its working memory to zero after every sentence and left the 4 `Experiential -> Phase / Synchronization` leaves (`Valence`, `Arousal`, `Resonance`, `Retention`) unused.
- **Design Choice**: Added `ContinuousMemoryState` in `src/lmft_lab/reflection_classifier.py`, which retains a decaying trace (`retention_decay = 0.65`) of active leaf coordinates across turns and computes `Resonance` (penalizing sudden disruption spikes). When a brief follow-up sentence in an ongoing scene is underspecified on its own but resonates ($\ge 0.55$) with recent memory, the retained trace carries forward without overriding sudden new events.
- **Result**: Verified on a live 3-turn GPU sequence (`experiments/run_continuous_tiered_demo.py`): a quiet post-rescue follow-up sentence improved from `0.00` (confused) statelessly to **`0.96` confidence/clarity** statefully, while a sudden earthquake on Turn 3 dropped `Resonance` to `0.31` and switched immediately to the earthquake rule (`0.80` confidence/clarity).

## D037: 24-Leaf Executive Controller (`Truth -> Theory / Fact`) vs. Raw 4B Baseline (2026-10-23)

- **Context**: To test whether the Fuzzy Tree should hold executive decision-making privileges over the 4B model, we connected the final two founding leaves (`psychological/truth/theory` and `psychological/truth/fact`) and built a closed-loop `ExecutiveDecision` controller (`COMMIT_FAST`, `VERIFY_HYPOTHESIS`, `ESCALATE_TO_TEACHER`).
- **Design Choice**: `Fact` measures how strongly the current sentence alone proves a rule (`min(raw_confidence, raw_clarity)`), while `Theory` measures working-memory carryover (`confidence - raw_confidence`) or partial single-frontrunner evidence. The resulting `ExecutiveDecision` constrains `Qwen3-4B`'s spoken response (`generate_executive_response`) and triggers 9B Teacher learning when `ESCALATE_TO_TEACHER` fires.
- **Result**: In a head-to-head GPU test (`experiments/run_executive_hybrid_eval.py`, `outputs/executive_hybrid_report.json`), the **24-Leaf LM/FT Hybrid scored `3/4`** (and automatically resolved the 4th unfamiliar bureaucratic-paperwork case via the 9B Teacher into `redundant_procedural_burden`), whereas **Raw `Qwen3-4B` Alone scored `0/4`** (losing the rescue context on Turn 2 and hallucinating `fair_structured_cooperation` on bureaucratic red tape instead of escalating).

## D038: Silent Subconscious (`STAY_SILENT`), Branch-Gated Child Querying, and Shoulder Fuzzy Rules (2026-11-01)

- **Context**: An always-talking observer interrupts peaceful routines and wastes GPU calls checking dormant Tier-2/Tier-3 child leaves. In Run #1 of our 6-turn operations stream (`outputs/silent_subconscious_step1_run1_failed_2of6.json`), the Hybrid scored **`2/6`** (losing to Raw `Qwen3-4B`'s **`3/6`**) because symmetric point-targets punished `1.00` agency against a `0.65` rule target, non-urgent peaceful rule overlap ignored `Execution == 0.0`, and working memory bled onto `0.22`-salience weather chatter.
- **Design Choice**: Added `STAY_SILENT` (skipping the speaking LLM call and outputting `""` when non-urgent and low-salience or `Execution < 0.20`), branch-gated Tier-2/Tier-3 drawer opening, upper/lower shoulder fuzzy curves (`>= 0.65` and `<= 0.30`), and active-leaf resonance filtering (`salience_estimate >= 0.35`, `continuity >= 0.35`).
- **Result**: On the unchanged 6-turn stream (`outputs/silent_subconscious_step1_report.json`), the Hybrid improved to **`4/6`** vs. Raw `Qwen3-4B`'s **`3/6`**, skipped **`50.0%` (`12/24`)** of child-leaf checks, nailed all 3 silent turns (`0` chars) plus Turn 6 bribery escalation (`1.00` Fact/Clarity after 9B Coach learning), and exposed untrained `Qwen3-4B` leaf-reading noise on Turns 3–4 as the exact target for Sleep-Mode instinct training.

## D039: Right-Brain Dominant 3-Bridge Equilibrium Loop, Self-Registry, and Will-to-Theory Circuit (2026-11-01)

- **Context**: Using `Qwen3-4B` as a one-shot 12-number JSON classifier produced lazy staircases, missed exclusionary rules on Beat 4, and forgot earlier story context on Beat 8 (calling a cracked hoarding wall an earthquake disaster). In addition, blending Left-Brain replies directly into raw sensory coordinates risked confusing internal thoughts about past diary entries with live outside `Fact`.
- **Design Choice**: Built `src/lmft_lab/right_brain_equilibrium.py` with 3 decoupled sensor bridge slots (`ThreeBridgeSensors`: Do-er `Will <-> Cultural`, Thinker `Truth <-> Philosophical`, Feeler `Contemplation <-> Experiential`—unlocking Tier-2/Tier-3 complex-feeling sub-drawers under `experiential/*` so rules can form multi-bridge chords rather than reducing feelings to a 1D happy/sad slider), an `InternalSelfRegistry` separating `EXTERNAL_WORLD` (`Fact`) from `INTERNAL_LEFT_BRAIN_THEORY` (`Theory`), and a directional circuit where `Will` sends outbound sleeve-tugs and `QwenLeftBrain` replies strictly into `Truth / Theory`.
- **Result**: Verified by 8 unit tests (`tests/test_right_brain_equilibrium.py`, `1,318` total passing tests) and a live 4-beat GPU check (`outputs/equilibrium_diary_smoke_report.json`): kept external `Fact` 100% immune to internal-thought pollution, eliminated Beat 1's staircase and Beat 8's false earthquake alarm, wrote scene-specific diary reflections, and exposed `Qwen3-4B`'s flat `Valence = 0.30` fallback scoring as the clear reason to train 3 dedicated fast neural bridge classifiers next.

## D040: Three Separate Neural Bridge Classifiers, Chord-Rhyming Tactical Hail, and Two-Layer Contemplation Ledger (2026-11-01)

- **Context**: Using `Qwen3-4B` for JSON coordinate scoring took several seconds per bridge and collapsed complex poetic feelings into a flat `Valence = 0.30`, while raw debug identifiers (`DOER_WILL_CULTURAL`, `unfamiliar_pattern_no_matching_rule`) cluttered the conscious Left Brain's prompt.
- **Design Choice**: Trained 3 separate `MiniLM-L12` cross-encoder classifiers (`src/lmft_lab/neural_bridge_classifiers.py`) on 60 non-parable scenes graded offline by `Qwen3.5-9B` (locking a 16-scene unseen holdout vault to disk before training), upgraded outbound `Will` prompts into a clean `TacticalHail` carrying pure `FeltChord` atmospheres and cross-memory Structural Rhymes & Chord Inversions (`src/lmft_lab/chord_memory.py`), and split `Contemplation` self-talk memory into an append-only `.jsonl` Raw Thought Stream plus a bounded `.json` Live Context Ledger (`src/lmft_lab/contemplation_ledger.py`).
- **Result**: All `1,322` tests pass; each neural bridge runs in `5.6–6.7 ms` on the RTX 4080 (`31–42 ms` full gated sensor pass) with unseen holdout Pearson `r = 0.69` (Do-er), `0.83` (Thinker), and `0.75` (Feeler). On the full 8-beat *"The Basin of Quiet Stone"* benchmark (`outputs/parable_soil_8beat_report.json`), valence spread widened to `0.34–0.80`, complex feeling chords tracked `austere_sanctuary -> hollow_stillness -> bittersweet_release`, Beat 8 paired a Beat 1 Rhyme with a Beat 5 Inversion (`0` false earthquake alarms), and two honest warts were flagged for Sleep Mode (subtle pious irony on Beat 6 and low zero-shot confidence on uncalibrated child traits).

## D041: 3-Branch Right-Brain Archives (Executive, Legislative, Judicial) and Dual-Hemisphere Self-Talk Competition (2026-11-01)

- **Context**: Calling `Qwen3-4B` whenever a scene needed a compound concept kept the system too dependent on slow LLM generation, while stateless social scoring missed ongoing enclosure on Beat 6 (`solitary_autarky`) and flagged a false fairness inversion on Beat 2 (`0.53` vs. `0.93`). In addition, an independent second-window audit caught four shortcuts in our initial `D041` draft (building candidate rules from the current scene's own coordinates, zeroing competing rules, harvesting unsettled drafts as ratified clauses, and clamping Beat 6's `fairness` to `0.22`).
- **Design Choice**: Built three persistent Right-Brain branch archives on disk (`src/lmft_lab/branch_archives.py`: Executive `cultural_history.json` with social-drift precedent tracking, Legislative `experiential_felt_diary.json`, and Judicial `philosophical_lexicon.json`) and stripped out all four shortcuts: candidate chords are built strictly from stored archive clause coordinates, competing rules are never zeroed out, unsettled scenes (`settled=False`) are never harvested as ratified clauses, and `social_drift` acts strictly as an Executive branch veto (`exec_vote=False`, `CONTEST_EXCLUSION`) without modifying raw sensor coordinates (`fairness = 0.51`).
- **Result**: All `1,325` unit tests pass (`tests/test_branch_archives.py`). On the un-rigged live GPU evaluation (`outputs/dual_hemisphere_soil_eval_results.json`), Epoch 1 settled **`4/8` (`[1, 2, 3, 7]`)**, fixed Beat 2's false inversion, and caught Beat 6's ongoing enclosure via an honest Executive branch veto (`[Exec=False, Leg=True, Jud=True]`) instead of misclassifying it as `solitary_autarky`; Epoch 2A settled **`5/8` (`[1, 2, 3, 7, 8]`)** with **`0` `Qwen3-4B` calls** (`58.8 ms`/beat, recombining Beat 1 + Beat 7 clauses on Beat 8 while leaving Beats 4–6 honestly unresolved until a Teacher teaches an exclusionary rule); and Epoch 2B (`0/4` settled, `61.8 ms`/beat) achieved `3-to-0` branch ratification on Unseen Beats 1 & 4 and caught `Social Drift = True` on Unseen Beat 3 while exposing Tier-1 rule ties and missing exclusionary rules as the exact target for Sleep-Mode 9B Teacher consolidation.

## D042: Offline 9B Sleep-Mode Consolidation (`sleep_consolidation.py`) for Unresolved Tensions and Rule Refinement (2026-11-01)

- **Context**: Without runtime shortcuts, the pre-Sleep system lacked a rule for non-violent exclusionary gatekeeping (leaving Parable Beats 4–6 and Unseen Bakehouse Beats 2–3 unresolved) and suffered from an underspecified `solitary_autarky` rule in `continuous_tiered_ruleset.json` (`needlessness: 0.45`, no `chaos` bound) that collided with communal sharing (`Unseen Beat 1`) and high-chaos wall fractures (`Beat 8`).
- **Design Choice**: Built `src/lmft_lab/sleep_consolidation.py` (`run_sleep_consolidation`), which runs offline after Epoch 1 using the local 9B Teacher (`Qwen3.5-9B-Q4_K_M.gguf` via `DirectGGUFTeacher`): it refines colliding Tier-1 rules in JSON (`solitary_autarky` tightened to `chaos: 0.10, needlessness: 0.75, self-sufficiency: 0.75, low: 0.72, high: 0.88`), groups unresolved wake-cycle scenes by coordinate signature, asks the 9B Teacher for domain-general rule descriptions and 3-branch clauses (strictly rejecting any parable or bakehouse words via `assert_domain_general_text`), anchors `target_leaves` to the Epoch 1 training coordinates, and persists `outputs/sleep_consolidated_ruleset.json` and updated `outputs/branch_archives/`.
- **Result**: All `1,328` unit tests pass (`tests/test_sleep_consolidation.py`). In the live GPU evaluation (`outputs/dual_hemisphere_soil_eval_results.json`), offline 9B Sleep Consolidation forged two domain-general rules (`exclusionary_enclosure_cluster` and `shared_access_to_renewal`) and raised 0-LLM-call performance from **`5/8` (`71.1 ms`) to `7/8` (`65.9 ms`)** on the 8-Beat Parable (leaving Beat 6 honestly in `CONTEST_EXCLUSION` via `social_drift` because its interior `Fair = 0.51` looks peaceful on static coordinates alone) and from **`1/4` (`53.9 ms`) to `4/4` (`46.7 ms`)** on the unseen 4-Beat Harbor Bakehouse.

## D043: Stage 1 of the 'Three-Legged Race' `<|subliminal_gap|>` Non-Verbal Embedding Splice (2026-11-02)

- **Context**: Translating the Right Brain's state into English stage directions in a prompt leaves `Qwen3-4B` acting like a detached essay writer in a relay race rather than a mind whose syntax and cadence are directly tugged by non-verbal emotional/structural chords.
- **Design Choice**: Outlined the 3-stage blueprint (`docs/SUBLIMINAL_GAP_SPLICE_BLUEPRINT.md`) and implemented Stage 1 (`src/lmft_lab/subliminal_gap_splice.py`): `SubliminalChordVector` organizes the Right Brain's live state into 4 strictly disjoint Departmental Vectors (`doer_vector` 17 floats, `thinker_vector` 16 floats, `feeler_vector` 16 floats, `cadence_vector` 3 floats) where active Tier-2/Tier-3 child leaves in each department are pooled into an 8-float departmental child chord (`pool_departmental_child_chord`) so sibling child leaves under the same parent branch (`austere_sanctuary`, `hollow_stillness`, `bittersweet_release`, or thousands of future leaves) remain 100% distinct without changing the vector size, `compute_cadence_rupture_drive` applies a `deadband = 0.45` so normal speech stays at `0.0`, and `SubliminalGapProjector` maps each disjoint department vector into its own virtual token embedding (`Do-er`, `Thinker`, `Feeler`, `Cadence/Rupture`) inside `<|subliminal_gap|>` (`inputs_embeds`).
- **Result**: Verified by 3 unit tests (`tests/test_subliminal_gap_splice.py`, `1,331` total passing tests), proving that sibling child leaves under `experiential/phase/*` (`austere_sanctuary`, `hollow_stillness`, `bittersweet_release`) produce 3 distinct `feeler_vector`s while leaving `doer_vector`, `thinker_vector`, and `cadence_vector` untouched, that 180 synthetic Tier-2/Tier-3 child leaves never change any department's vector size, that zero indices overlap across the 4 virtual tokens, and that swapping only the non-verbal Departmental Chords inside `<|subliminal_gap|>` flips a causal PyTorch Transformer's post-gap token prediction from smooth prose to high-rupture `M-A-C-H-I-N-E` cadence (`> 95%` confidence).

## D044: Stage 2 (`2A` & `2B`) Offline Contrastive `<|subliminal_gap|>` Trajectory Schema & 9B+MiniLM Dataset Generation (2026-11-02)

- **Context**: Before training `SubliminalGapProjector` + LoRA on `Qwen3-4B` (Stage 3), we needed a strict data contract (`Sub-Step 2A`) and an offline matched-pair generator (`Sub-Step 2B`) where identical opening clauses (`clause_1_prefix`) branch into two opposite second clauses (`continuation_a` vs. `continuation_b`) purely from the 52-float Right-Brain chord inside `<|subliminal_gap|>`, without ever keeping `Qwen3.5-9B` and `Qwen3-4B` in VRAM at the same time.
- **Design Choice**: Built `src/lmft_lab/gap_trajectory_schema.py` (`ContrastiveGapPair`, `MIN_CHORD_L2_DISTANCE = 0.35`, `validate_train_holdout_disjointness`, and `compile_chess_posture_chords` mapping the 6 Chess pieces to the 6 Philosophical leaves) and `src/lmft_lab/gap_trajectory_generator.py` (`experiments/run_generate_gap_trajectories.py`), which calls the real `Qwen3.5-9B-Q4_K_M.gguf` Teacher (`DirectGGUFTeacher`) and scores/verifies every continuation with the real 3 PyTorch MiniLM-L12 Bridge Classifiers (`models/right_brain_bridges/*`) with zero pre-written fallback strings. Also added `anchor_weights: tuple[float, ...] | None = None` to `LearnedLeaf` (`score_leaf_anchor_weights_at_birth`) and purged unit-test strings from `DEPARTMENT_FOUNDING_ANCHORS`.
- **Result**: All 10 new unit tests in `tests/test_gap_trajectory_schema.py` and `tests/test_gap_trajectory_generator.py` pass (`1,341` total passing tests). In the live offline generation run (`outputs/gap_trajectory_generation_report.json`, `53.72 s`), **`17/18` training seeds** (`outputs/gap_trajectories_train.jsonl`, `mean L2 = 1.9055`) and **`6/6` unseen holdout seeds** (`outputs/gap_trajectories_holdout.jsonl`, `mean L2 = 2.1851`) passed all structural and 3-bridge MiniLM sensor checks (`14` first-try passes, `10` retries, and `1` seed `train-tone-01` openly rejected after 3 attempts rather than using a hardcoded fallback), and `Qwen3.5-9B` + CUDA cache were completely unloaded at completion.

## D045: Sub-Step 3 PyTorch `Qwen/Qwen3-4B` Left-Brain LoRA + Corpus Callosum Training & Holdout Falsification (2026-11-02)

- **Context**: Because `Qwen3-4B-Q4_K_M.gguf` is a `llama.cpp` GGUF file that cannot accept spliced PyTorch `inputs_embeds` or backpropagate gradients, Sub-Step 3 needed real HuggingFace PyTorch `safetensors` weights (`Qwen/Qwen3-4B`), a clean Windows-native `LoRALinear` adapter wrapper (`q_proj, k_proj, v_proj, o_proj`), `-100` pre-gap label masking (`build_post_gap_only_labels`), and the 3 un-rigged holdout falsification checks (`Check A`, `Check B`, `Check C`).
- **Design Choice**: Built `src/lmft_lab/train_left_brain_lora.py` and `experiments/run_train_left_brain_lora.py`, which load `Qwen/Qwen3-4B` in `torch.bfloat16` (`gradient_checkpointing_enable()`, `use_cache=False`, `batch_size=1`), wrap the top 18 transformer layers (`72` linear projections) with `LoRALinear(rank=16, lora_alpha=32.0)`, mask all tokens before `<|subliminal_gap|>` and all 4 virtual gap tokens with `-100`, train `SubliminalGapProjector` + `LoRALinear` for 6 epochs on the 17 training pairs, save the checkpoint to `models/left_brain_lora/` (`sleep_cycle_index = 1`), and evaluate on the 6 unseen holdout pairs with the real 3-bridge MiniLM classifiers.
- **Result**: All 4 unit tests in `tests/test_train_left_brain_lora.py` pass (`1,345` total passing tests), and training peaked at **`8.127 GB` VRAM** on the 16 GB RTX 4080 (`total_loss` dropped from `4.8622` to `0.7557`). On the 6 unseen holdout pairs (`outputs/left_brain_lora_eval_report.json`): **Check A** achieved **`5/6` positive chord-swap log-prob shift** (`mean_net_chord_swap_margin = +0.7775` vs. `+0.0435` pre-training; raw un-normalized `both_branches_flipped = 0/6` because `Qwen3-4B`'s frozen base prior gives one candidate sentence lower baseline NLL than the other), **Check B** (`max_unplugged_chord_swap_shift`) was **`0.000000`**, and **Check C** flipped greedy Clause 2 continuations on **`5/6` unseen holdout prompts** (`mean_generated_sensor_l2 = 0.9711`)—including switching `holdout-chess-02` from `"We play Bf6 and Kg7 to neutralize the Knight threat and protect the King."` (`Chord A`) to `"leap with Knight xg7+ followed by Qxg7# to win instantly."` (`Chord B`) and eliminating `Epoch 0`'s untrained Mandarin glitch on `holdout-tone-02`.
